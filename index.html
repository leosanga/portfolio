Lead Qualification & Routing System (HubSpot)

Problem

Inbound leads arrived from many different sources — website, course signups, email outreach, a separate CRM, content downloads — and were manually reviewed and routed, causing inconsistent follow-up timing and mismatched sales paths.

Automation

Built a workflow in HubSpot that automatically segments and routes all leads regardless of source, qualifies them against defined criteria, and triggers the correct sales sequence and deal assignment.

Result

Improved lead routing accuracy and consistency, reduced manual lead triage, and increased engagement through faster, targeted follow-up.
